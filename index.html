About Us

More Than Just a Bake

The Bake Factory brings together freshly baked goodness, hearty breakfasts, comforting café classics, indulgent desserts and carefully crafted coffee in a warm and welcoming space.

Located in Gachibowli, Hyderabad, our bakery café is designed to be a cozy place to enjoy good food with friends and family.
