Who We Are

A Sanctuary Built for Good Food & Warmth

The Bake Factory was born out of a simple passion: to bring authentic artisanal bread, specialty coffee, and handcrafted comfort food to Gachibowli.

Every morning before dawn, our master bakers knead organic unbleached flour, water, and sea salt into slow-fermented sourdoughs. Paired with freshly pulled Baba Budan Arabica espresso shots, every visit is designed to feel like a warm hug.

  • 24-Hour Sourdough Fermentation
  • Freshly Roasted Arabica Espresso
  • Stone-Fired Italian Gourmet Pizzas
  • Cozy Ambience & High-Speed WiFi

Our Culinary Pillars

Simple Ingredients. Uncompromised Craft.

Everything we serve is built on four core principles of freshness, quality, and hospitality.

Artisanal Ingredients

We source organic unbleached flour, pure French butter, and farm-fresh produce locally to ensure peak flavor in every bite.

Baked Fresh Daily

No shortcuts or artificial preservatives. Our loaves, paninis, and pastries are baked fresh every morning from scratch.

Specialty Coffee

Sourced from legendary South Indian coffee estates, expertly roasted and pulled by trained baristas for smooth perfection.

Warm Hospitality

A relaxed, neighborhood atmosphere with soft warm lighting, cozy seating, and attentive service for work or dates.
